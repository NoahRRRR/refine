Privacy Policy

Last updated September 10, 2026

REFINE ("we," "our," "the app") is a course platform. This page explains what information the app and website collect and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Course password. To access the course, you enter a shared password. It's checked against a fixed value on our server — we don't create individual accounts, and we don't store the password you type.
  • Session cookie. After a successful login, we set a single cookie that proves you're logged in (your role — student or admin — and an expiration time, cryptographically signed). It's used only to keep you logged in for up to 30 days and contains no personal information.

What we don't collect

  • No names, emails, or account profiles
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No location data
  • No data sold or shared with third parties

Cookies

The only cookie we set is the session cookie described above. It's required for the course to function (you can't stay logged in without it) and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Data retention

We don't maintain a database of users. The session cookie expires automatically after 30 days or when you log out, whichever comes first.

Children's privacy

REFINE is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the date above.
